# Scientific Calculator
## How to Use
* **Advanced Functions:** Click the **"adv/basic"** buton to toggle between stadard arithmetic and advanced scientific operations.
* **Getting Answers/Viewing Final result:** Press the **'='** button after entering your expression to compute and view the final result.
* **Inverse Trigonometric Functions:** Click the **"2nd"** button to toggle the trig keys to their invese functions.
* **Angle Mode:** Use the **"Deg/Rad"** toggle to switch the angular measurement unit between Degrees and Radians for all trigonomeric calcuations.
* **Using Previous Answers:** Click the **"ans"** button to recall and insert the result of your most recent calculation into a new expression.
